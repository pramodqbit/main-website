Four phases, each ending in something you can read, not just a status meeting. You always know what we decided and why.

PHASE 1

Discover

We sit with the people doing the work and write down what actually slows them down.

You getProblem brief · success metrics
PHASE 2

Decide

We weigh the options on paper, including the ones we reject, before writing code.

You getDecision log · architecture · estimate
PHASE 3

Build

Two-week sprints, a demo every sprint, and a written update every Friday in your time zone.

You getWorking software · weekly log
PHASE 4

Measure

We check the metrics we agreed in Phase 1 and publish the results, good or bad.

LOG / FAQ
How do you work across US and European time zones?
We keep at least four hours of overlap with your working day, send a written update every Friday, and record every sprint demo so nobody waits on a meeting.
How long does a typical project take?
Our recent projects ran 10–16 weeks from discovery to launch. You get a written estimate at the end of the Decide phase, before any build work is committed.
What does it cost?
Fixed price for well-defined scopes, time and materials for evolving products, or a dedicated team for long-term work. Every quote is itemised.
Can you take over an existing codebase?
Yes. We start with a two-week audit and hand you a written log of what we found and what we’d fix first.
